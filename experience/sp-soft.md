# SP Soft

**Role:** Co-Founder / Software Engineer

**Employment Type:** Full-time

**Duration:** May 2018 – Dec 2022

---

## Work Context

I co-founded SP Soft with a backend engineer. In our two-person team, I worked directly with customers from discovery and on-site workflow analysis through solution design, implementation, testing and delivery. I personally designed the user interfaces and user experiences and developed the frontend applications, while API design and integration were shared with my backend colleague.

We accepted projects only when we were confident they aligned with our capabilities and could be delivered to an appropriate standard. For accepted projects, our involvement covered requirements analysis, solution design, software architecture, implementation, deployment, and long-term maintenance.

During this period, I worked on software products across healthcare, hospitality, tourism, education, finance, and business management. Each project introduced different operational workflows and constraints, requiring rapid domain analysis and architecture adapted to the specific problem.

Near the end of this period, I also joined a different team to contribute analysis to a tourism platform. That collaboration was separate from the products delivered by our two-person team.

## Responsibilities

My responsibilities covered technical discovery, solution design, frontend engineering, and customer collaboration throughout the software delivery lifecycle.

I was responsible for:

- Leading customer discovery and analyzing business workflows.
- Visiting customer workplaces, researching competitors and identifying whether target users primarily worked on mobile or desktop.
- Evaluating technical feasibility and defining suitable solution approaches.
- Translating business requirements into implementation plans.
- Designing software architecture and frontend application structure.
- Designing product UI/UX around customer workflows and target devices.
- Developing maintainable frontend applications across diverse projects.
- Collaborating with backend engineers on API design and system integration.
- Supporting deployment, customer feedback, and long-term product evolution.
- Testing products, evaluating web performance with tools such as GTmetrix, and publishing applications to international stores.

## Key Engineering Work

- **Technical Discovery & Feasibility Assessment:** Worked directly with customers to understand operational needs, evaluate whether proposed products aligned with the team’s capabilities, and define realistic technical approaches before development began.

- **Domain-Specific Solution Design:** Designed software solutions across multiple industries, adapting workflows and architectural decisions to each domain rather than applying a predefined product model.

- **Frontend Architecture:** Established maintainable frontend structures for products with significantly different requirements, supporting continued development and product evolution after initial delivery.

- **Product and Experience Design:** Combined customer interviews, workplace visits and competitor research to define workflows and UI/UX. Adapted mobile and desktop priorities to the product's actual audience.

- **Esports and Commerce:** Analyzed and developed a platform combining player profiles, tournaments and a store for physical, customizable and digital products. Worked on purchasing and fulfillment workflows and Steam API integration for inventory, sales, item transfers and gifts.

- **Tourism Analysis Collaboration:** Contributed analysis with another project team for a platform covering tours, accommodation and multiple participant/provider roles. Helped work through structured multi-day itineraries and payment, cancellation and settlement scenarios.

- **Quality and Release:** Tested delivered products and assessed web performance with GTmetrix and similar services. Developed and published several applications to international stores.
